# Research contracts

Preserve mean-loss normalization, all registered parameters, residual identities,
shared global probes, and identical damping/metric/scope in interaction ablations.
Run relevant numerical, non-mutation and replay tests after algorithm changes.
Keep exact references, restricted oracles, sketches and unconverged solves distinct.
Never relabel a failed check or change a protocol retrospectively to favor a result.
Use precise connected scientific prose and verified source/dependency provenance.
Follow docs/writing_style.md; its language rules do not freeze numerical conclusions.
Keep this repository independent. Do not download data, run sweeps, publish, or
launch expensive training without a deliberately selected experiment.
Read HANDOFF.md when resuming research. After a completed or failed experiment,
update its ledger, findings and active-work status, link the raw run and report,
and update STATUS.md. Keep prospective protocols and negative results intact.
