# Scientific writing

Use precise connected prose. Separate identities, approximations, observations and
hypotheses. Name the batch, scope, partition, damping and search policy for every
comparison. Describe settings as used or prospectively selected, not globally optimal.
Report a failed approximation without changing the protocol to obtain a positive plot.

Low exposure alone is not an optimization objective. A large cross term does not
establish exploitable improvement. PSD does not establish curvature accuracy;
same-batch improvement does not establish independent-batch improvement. A toy
classification result does not establish language-model efficiency. Avoid undefined
metaphors such as routing or shared modes. These language rules do not freeze numerical
conclusions and should be revised when evidence warrants more precise wording.
